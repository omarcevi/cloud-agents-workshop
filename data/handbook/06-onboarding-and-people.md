# Onboarding and people

Kuzey Labs is a fictional company. Everything in this handbook is made up for a workshop.

## Your first week

- Day 1: laptop handover, SSO setup and a welcome lunch with your team.
- Day 2–3: security training (mandatory, about 2 hours) and the engineering or business onboarding track.
- Day 5: first 1:1 with your team lead.

## Teams

| Team | What they do | Offices |
|---|---|---|
| Platform | Cloud infrastructure, CI/CD, on-call | Istanbul, Berlin |
| Data | Analytics, data pipelines, ML | Ankara |
| Product | Product management and design | Istanbul |
| IT Desk | Laptops, access, internal tools | Istanbul |
| People | HR, People Partners, recruiting | Istanbul |
| Sales | Customers in DACH and Turkey | Berlin, Istanbul |

## People Partners

Every team has a People Partner for questions about leave, growth, pay and conflicts. You can find yours
in your HRIS profile.

## Feedback and growth

Kuzey Labs runs growth conversations twice a year, in **March** and **September**. Promotions are decided
in March together with pay reviews.
