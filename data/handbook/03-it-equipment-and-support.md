# IT equipment and support

Kuzey Labs is a fictional company. Everything in this handbook is made up for a workshop.

## Laptops

- Engineers get a laptop refresh every **3 years**; everyone else every **4 years**.
- Report a lost or stolen laptop **immediately** as a P1 ticket and tell Security.
- Do not install unapproved software. The approved list is in the IT portal.

## Opening an IT ticket

Open tickets through the IT desk (or ask the Workplace Assistant). Include what you were doing, what you
expected and what happened. Pick a category: `HARDWARE`, `SOFTWARE`, `ACCESS`, `NETWORK` or `OTHER`.

## Priorities and response times

| Priority | Meaning | First response |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | You cannot work at all, or a security incident | 30 minutes |
| P2 | A major part of your work is blocked | 4 working hours |
| P3 | Something is broken but there is a workaround | 1 working day |
| P4 | A request or question | 3 working days |

## Passwords and access

- Reset your SSO password yourself at the SSO portal. The IT desk never asks for your password.
- Access to production systems needs your team lead's approval in the ticket (`ACCESS` category).
- VPN is only needed for the legacy finance systems. Everything else uses SSO and device trust.

## Peripherals

Every office has a stock of keyboards, mice, headsets and USB-C adapters. Ask the IT desk with a P4
ticket. Monitors for home offices are covered by the home office budget, not by IT.
