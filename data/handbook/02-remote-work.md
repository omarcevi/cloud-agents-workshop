# Remote and hybrid work

Kuzey Labs is a fictional company. Everything in this handbook is made up for a workshop.

## Hybrid schedule

Kuzey Labs is hybrid. Each office has two **anchor days** when teams are expected in the office:

| Office | Anchor days |
|---|---|
| Istanbul | Tuesday and Thursday |
| Ankara | Monday and Wednesday |
| Berlin | Tuesday and Wednesday |

Outside anchor days you choose where you work. Agree exceptions with your team lead.

## Working from another country

You can work from another country for up to **20 working days per calendar year**. Request it in the HRIS
at least 2 weeks in advance so the People team can check tax and visa rules. Working from abroad is not
leave and does not reduce your leave balance.

## Home office budget

New joiners get a one-time home office budget of **EUR 600** (or the TRY equivalent) for a chair, desk or
monitor. Submit receipts as an expense within 90 days of your start date.

## Core hours

Core collaboration hours are **10:00–16:00 in your office's time zone**. Outside core hours, nobody should
expect an immediate reply.
