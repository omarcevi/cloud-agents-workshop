# Leave policy

Kuzey Labs is a fictional company. Everything in this handbook is made up for a workshop.

## Annual leave entitlement

| Office | Annual leave (working days) | Extra days after 5 years |
|---|---|---|
| Istanbul | 20 | +2 |
| Ankara | 20 | +2 |
| Berlin | 28 | +1 |

Entitlement is pro-rated in your first calendar year. Your current balance is always in the HRIS; the
Workplace Assistant reads it for you.

## Requesting leave

- Request leave in the HRIS (or ask the Workplace Assistant to submit it for you).
- Give at least **3 working days' notice** for requests of 1–2 days, and **2 weeks' notice** for longer requests.
- Your **team lead approves** your request. The HRIS routes it automatically based on your team.
- A request is `PENDING` until approved. Pending days count against your balance.
- Half days are allowed. Use leave type `ANNUAL_HALF_DAY`.

## Carry-over

You can carry up to **5 unused days** into the next year. Carried-over days expire on **31 March**.

## Sick leave

Tell your team lead on the first day. From the third consecutive day you need a doctor's note uploaded to
the HRIS. Sick leave does not reduce your annual leave balance.

## Parental leave

Parental leave follows local law in each office, plus 4 extra paid weeks from Kuzey Labs for every parent.
Talk to your People Partner at least 8 weeks before the planned start.

## Public holidays

Public holidays depend on your **office**, not on where you happen to be working.

### Istanbul and Ankara (2026)

- 1 January — New Year's Day
- 23 April — National Sovereignty and Children's Day
- 1 May — Labour and Solidarity Day
- 19 May — Commemoration of Atatürk, Youth and Sports Day
- 15 July — Democracy and National Unity Day
- 30 August — Victory Day
- 29 October — Republic Day (28 October afternoon is a half day)
- Ramadan Feast and Feast of Sacrifice: dates are announced in the HRIS each year

### Berlin (2026)

- 1 January, Good Friday, Easter Monday, 1 May, Ascension Day, Whit Monday
- 3 October — Day of German Unity
- 25 and 26 December

## Platform team on-call

Platform team members who are on call during a requested leave day must find a swap before the team lead
approves the request. The on-call rota is in the platform team's calendar.
