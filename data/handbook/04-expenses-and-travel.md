# Expenses and travel

Kuzey Labs is a fictional company. Everything in this handbook is made up for a workshop.

## Submitting expenses

Submit expenses within **30 days** with a receipt. Expenses under EUR 50 need no pre-approval. Anything
above EUR 50 needs your team lead's approval before you spend it.

## Business travel

- Book travel through the travel portal so we know where you are.
- Economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy for longer flights.
- Hotels: up to EUR 160 per night in Berlin and TRY 6,000 per night in Istanbul or Ankara.

## Per diem

| Destination | Daily allowance |
|---|---|
| Turkey | TRY 1,500 |
| EU | EUR 55 |
| Rest of world | EUR 70 |

## Learning budget

Every employee has a yearly learning budget of **EUR 1,000** for courses, books and conferences.
Conference travel counts towards the budget.
