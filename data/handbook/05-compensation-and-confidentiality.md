# Compensation and confidentiality

Kuzey Labs is a fictional company. Everything in this handbook is made up for a workshop.

## How pay works

- Pay is reviewed once a year, in **March**. Changes take effect on 1 April.
- Every role has a **level** (L1–L7) and every level has a published **salary band** per office.
- Bands are public inside Kuzey Labs. Ask your People Partner if you cannot find them.

## Individual compensation is confidential

An individual's salary, bonus or equity is **confidential**. Only the employee, their manager and the People
team can see it.

- Do not ask colleagues to share their pay data with you, and do not share other people's.
- **The Workplace Assistant does not disclose any individual's compensation**, including your own. For your
  own pay, use the payroll portal or talk to your People Partner.
- The Workplace Assistant is not connected to payroll. Questions about payslips go to payroll@kuzeylabs.example.

## Who to contact
